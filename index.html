How it works

Three clicks, no setup

1

Open the page

Your Amazon orders, your Kindle notebook or a Trello board — signed in as usual.

2

Click the extension

It reads what you already see, including the next pages, and shows a preview.

3

Save the files

PDF invoices, CSV for Excel and Google Sheets, JSON, or Markdown for Obsidian and Notion.

Privacy

FAQ

Questions

Which Amazon stores are supported?

All Amazon marketplaces that have the "Your Orders" page — .com, .co.uk, .de, .fr, .it, .es, .pl, .nl, .se, .ca, .com.au and others. PDF invoices are saved where Amazon issues them; for other orders you get the order summary.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Install the extension and use it. For Pro you only paste the license key you get after paying.

Lifetime or monthly — what's the difference?

Lifetime is a one-time payment and includes future updates. Monthly renews each month and can be cancelled any time from the link in your Paddle receipt; Pro stays active until the end of the paid month.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — full refund within 14 days, no questions asked. See the Refund Policy.

I lost my license key.

Email sergefrontend@gmail.com from the address you paid with and we'll send it again.

A site changed and the extension stopped working.

It happens — Amazon, Kindle and Trello redesign their pages. Click "Report a problem" in the extension and email us the file; we usually fix it within days.
