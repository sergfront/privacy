Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06

This policy covers the browser extensions (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) Invoice Downloader for Amazon, Highlights Exporter for Kindle and Board Exporter for Trello — each called "the extension" below — and this website.

What the extension stores

Everything below stays in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage).

Page content

The extension reads the page of the supported site you are on (your Amazon orders, your Kindle Notebook, a Trello board) only when you click its button. What it reads is shown in the popup and saved only to files you choose to export or download. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

To load the same data the page shows, the extension may request the site's own pages and endpoints with your existing session: Amazon (the next pages of your orders and each order's invoices), Kindle (your notebook's highlight pages), Trello (the board's JSON). It never sees your passwords.

Problem reports

If you click "Report a problem", the extension saves a file to your computer with the structure of the page (HTML tags and class names). Text, numbers, names, IDs and form values are removed. Nothing is sent automatically — you decide whether to email that file to us.

License checks

Only when you activate, check (once a day) or unlink a license, the extension sends the license key, the activation ID and the random device name to our license server (a Cloudflare Worker operated by us). The server checks that the purchase behind the key is paid and not refunded, and stores, per license, the list of activated devices (activation ID, device name, date) to enforce the 3-device limit. We do not log IP addresses; Cloudflare processes network data to deliver the request under its privacy policy. In Firefox, the extension asks for your permission before sending the license key.

Purchases

Card payments are taken on the secure page of monobank (Universal Bank, Ukraine), which processes your card details under its own privacy policy. We never see your card number. For each order our server keeps the product, the amount, the order code, the date and, only if you enter it, your email (to resend a lost key). From the bank we receive the amount, the time and the comment of the payment, and the payer name the bank shows, to match the payment to your order and for our records. If you pay in USDT, we keep the network, the transaction hash and the amount; blockchain transfers are public by nature.

This website

No analytics, cookies of our own or tracking.

When you click a "Buy" button we count the click — only the product, the plan and the month, without your IP address or any identifier — so we know which Pro versions people want.

Pro waitlist. If you leave your email on the "Pro is launching soon" page, we store it with the product name, use it only to tell you once that Pro is available, and delete the list after that. To be removed earlier, email us.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, tracking, ads or third-party scripts in the extension.
  • We do not collect browsing history or page content, and we do not sell or share data.
  • No remote code: everything the extension runs ships inside the extension package.

Keeping and removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored. To free a device slot first, open the extension's Settings and click Unlink this device, which also deletes that activation from our server. Paid order records are kept as long as the license is valid and as required by law; unpaid orders are deleted after 3 days. To ask us to delete the activation records for your license, email us.
